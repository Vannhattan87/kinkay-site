The fine print

What is and is not included

  • Hair is always included. Every price on this page covers makeup and hair together. Kay does not sell makeup on its own.
  • Travel inside Ho Chi Minh City is included, along with touch-ups. Kay comes to your apartment, hotel or venue.
  • Outside the city is quoted in full before you confirm. Da Nang, Hoi An, Nha Trang, Phu Quoc, Da Lat — travel and accommodation stated up front, nothing added afterwards.
  • A deposit holds the date. The amount and the change or cancellation terms are confirmed before you transfer anything.
  • Team shoots are invoiced. Company bookings can be invoiced to the business with the usual details. Ask when you book and Kay will confirm what she needs.
  • Roughly, in dollars. At about 26,000 VND to the US dollar: a headshot is around 58 USD, an event look around 70 USD, a team half day around 173 USD, a team full day around 288 USD, a full wedding day around 190 USD. Pay in dong — the conversion is only here so the numbers mean something at a glance.
