Who this is for

The events an expat calendar in Saigon actually has

There is a rhythm to this city if you work here. Chamber galas cluster in the last quarter. Company year-end parties land in December and January. School fundraisers, awards nights, and the wedding of a colleague you barely know but must look right at.

  • Kay comes to you. Your apartment, your hotel room, or the venue. Anywhere inside Ho Chi Minh City is in the price — you are not crossing town in traffic with your hair done.
  • Makeup and hair in one session. Ninety minutes to two hours. Hair is always included; Kay does not sell makeup on its own.
  • Built for photographs and long nights. Ballroom lighting is unforgiving and the night runs long. The base is set to survive both and still read as skin at close range.
  • Tell her the finish time, not the start time. Kay works backwards from when you need to walk out the door.

Practical

Things worth knowing before the night

Book a week or two out

Weekend evenings in October through January fill up. Weekdays are usually open at shorter notice, and they are cheaper.

Send a photo of the dress

Neckline and colour change the look more than most people expect. One photo on Zalo saves a conversation.

Say if you will be photographed

Press wall, company photographer, or a stage. Flash and stage light need a different base than a dinner table does.

Skin first

No new treatments in the week before. Nothing ruins an event face faster than skin that is still recovering from a facial.
