Worth knowing

Things nobody tells you before an áo dài shoot

It starts before sunrise

The good locations — the Central Post Office, Bến Thành, the old colonial streets, the university — are empty and beautifully lit for about an hour after sunrise, and crowded and harsh after that. A six o'clock shoot means makeup at four thirty. Plan the night before accordingly.

Saigon humidity is the real enemy

You will be outdoors, moving, in a fitted high-collared garment, in heat. The base has to be set for that from the start — this is not the same application as an air-conditioned dinner, and it is the single most common reason rental-shop makeup slides off by the second location.

The áo dài decides the hair

The collar sits high and the silhouette is long and narrow. Hair worn down fights it in almost every frame. Send Kay a photo of the áo dài when you book and the hair is planned around it rather than improvised on the morning.

Book the makeup before the photographer

Photographers in this city have more availability than good makeup artists do at sunrise. If you have a fixed date, lock the face first.
