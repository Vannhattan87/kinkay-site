The day itself

What a Vietnamese wedding day actually involves

If this is your first one, it is not one event. It is two, and they are very different. Nobody tells foreign brides this until the week before.

Morning — the ancestral and tea ceremony

At the family home, traditional, usually in áo dài, and it starts early. Five or six in the morning is normal because the hour is chosen to be auspicious. The room is small, the light is warm, and there are a lot of relatives and a lot of photographs at close range. The makeup has to look correct in a photo taken from one metre away.

Evening — the reception

A hotel or restaurant ballroom, western-style, a different dress and a different look. Stage lighting, a long night, hundreds of guests. Kay comes back between the two events to change the look completely — this is not a touch-up, it is a second face for a second occasion.

Why the full-day tier costs what it costs

Because it is two full applications separated by eight hours, plus re-touching on the spot through both, plus the whole day held on the calendar. The ancestral-ceremony-only tier exists for couples who are genuinely not having an evening reception. If there is a reception, it falls under the full day.

Everyone else in the photos

Mothers, bridesmaids, sisters, and the tea-ceremony tray bearers — the young relatives who carry the gift trays in the morning. They all appear in the same photographs as you. Tell Kay the headcount in advance so she brings assistants and nobody is waiting in a queue at five in the morning.

Timing

When to book

Peak season

October through February, plus any date a family has chosen as auspicious. Book two to four months out. Good dates go first and they go early.

Off-peak

Four to six weeks is usually enough. Weekdays are often open at shorter notice.

Trial

Three to six weeks before the wedding. Close enough that your skin is in its wedding condition, far enough that there is time to change your mind.

Flying in

Tell Kay your arrival date. If you land two days before, the trial happens on the ground — say so early so the calendar holds both.
