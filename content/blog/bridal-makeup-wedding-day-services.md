# BRIDAL MAKEUP — WEDDING DAY SERVICES

One of the most common questions we receive from brides is:

**“How much does bridal makeup cost for the wedding day?”**

Every wedding is different, and so are the moments a bride needs to prepare for. At KINKAY, we offer different Bridal Makeup services depending on your ceremony, schedule and level of support you would like throughout the day.

### 01. FAMILY CEREMONY MAKEUP — 3,000,000 VND

For the traditional family ceremony, we create an **elegant, refined and effortless makeup look** that complements the intimate nature of the occasion.

As the ceremony is usually shorter, the bride can be fully prepared for the event before our team leaves after completing the makeup and final touch-ups.

**Price: 3,000,000 VND**

***

### 02. WEDDING DAY BRIDAL MAKEUP — 5,000,000 VND

Your wedding day requires a different level of preparation.

From getting ready and the ceremony to photography and the reception, your makeup needs to remain beautiful and comfortable through a long and eventful day.

For this reason, KINKAY takes a more detailed approach to the wedding-day look, carefully considering **your skin, the weather, the duration of wear, your dress, styling and the overall aesthetic of your wedding.**

If your chosen look requires specific accessories or additional details, we encourage you to discuss them with us in advance so that everything can be thoughtfully prepared and coordinated.

**Price: 5,000,000 VND**

***

### 03. BRIDAL TAKE CARE — 2,000,000 VND / DAY

For brides who would like to have dedicated beauty support throughout the day, KINKAY offers **Bridal Take Care**.

Our team stays with you throughout your wedding day to help maintain your appearance and take care of any beauty-related details that may arise.

**Take Care includes:**

• Makeup touch-ups when needed
• Refreshing areas affected by heat, humidity, sweat or tears
• Adjusting or changing the makeup look when required
• Overall beauty and appearance touch-ups
• Assistance when changing wedding dresses
• Helping you remain polished and photo-ready throughout the day

**Price: 2,000,000 VND / day**

For brides getting married in Ho Chi Minh City, we highly recommend considering Take Care if you want a more relaxed and seamless experience.

A wedding day can be unpredictable. The weather may be hot, emotions may bring tears, and changing dresses throughout the day can naturally affect your makeup and overall appearance.

With Take Care, you do not have to worry about these details. You can simply be present, enjoy your celebration and create memories with the people you love — while our team takes care of keeping you looking your best.

***

### BRIDES OUTSIDE HO CHI MINH CITY

Our Bridal Makeup service rates remain the same for brides outside Ho Chi Minh City.

Additional **transportation and accommodation costs for the team** will apply depending on the location and schedule.

All additional expenses will be discussed clearly in advance, so there are no unexpected costs for the bride.

***

### “WILL MY MAKEUP LAST ALL DAY?”

This is one of the questions brides ask us most often.

Beautiful bridal makeup is not only about how it looks when it is first completed. It is also about **longevity, comfort and how well it adapts to your skin and the conditions of your wedding day.**

At KINKAY, we carefully select products and techniques based on each bride’s skin type, the weather and the length of time the makeup needs to be worn.

However, we believe in being honest with our brides.

**No makeup can remain completely unchanged throughout an entire wedding day.**

Heat, humidity, sweat, tears, movement and long hours of celebration can naturally affect makeup over time. This is completely normal.

Our goal is not simply to create makeup that looks beautiful when you first see yourself in the mirror.

**Our goal is to create a bridal look that is thoughtfully designed for your entire experience — and to be there when you need us.**

For brides who want complete peace of mind and dedicated support throughout the day, **Bridal Take Care is highly recommended.**

Because your wedding day is about more than looking beautiful.

**It is about feeling confident, cared for and completely present in every unforgettable moment.**
